# Governance

## Decision-making

The Metadatastician is maintained by Jonathan D.A. Jewell (`@hyperpolymath`). Decisions on
scope, architecture and releases rest with the maintainer.

Some repositories in this organisation are **co-developed with family**, and are licensed
`AGPL-3.0-or-later` for that reason. Where a repository has a co-developer, decisions about
that work — including any change of licence — require their agreement, not the
maintainer's alone.

## Licensing decisions

Licence and provenance changes are **manual and owner-only**. They are never performed by
automation, scripts, or sweeps. This rule exists because prior automated attempts
misattributed third-party authorship, which is a legal-integrity problem rather than a
tidiness one.
